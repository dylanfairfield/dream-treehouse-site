Dream Treehouse

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Dream Treehouse is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Fresh Field, LLC ("we" and "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read by parents and players alike.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • No ads. Nothing is used to track you or sold to anyone.
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics (like which level was played and whether it was won) so we can make the game better.
  • Your progress is saved on your iPhone.
  • Apple handles every purchase. We never see payment details.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, the treehouse you've built, and which purchases have already been delivered) and your settings (music, sounds, haptics, reduce motion, the lives reminder) are saved on your iPhone. They aren't sent to us. Like any app's data, they're included in your iPhone's own backups. Deleting the game deletes them.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To learn which levels are too hard, where players stop, and whether new features work, the game sends a short record of what happens in the game to PostHog, an analytics service that stores it for us in the United States. These records are:

  • A play session starting.
  • A level starting and ending: the level number, whether it was won, moves left, how long it took, specials made, boosters used and extra moves bought.
  • Extra moves offered and bought, boosters used, treehouse pieces built, areas finished, lesson steps seen, the shop opened, and which coin pack was bought (never payment details).
  • Basic technical details sent with each record, such as the game's version and build, the kind of device and its model and screen size, the iOS version, and the language and time zone settings.
  • A random ID made when the game is first opened, so records from the same install can be grouped. It isn't connected to your name, your Apple Account or your iPhone's advertising identifier, and deleting the game resets it.

We've set PostHog up so that it doesn't store IP addresses, doesn't look up location, and doesn't record the screen or taps. We don't send names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location or the advertising identifier.

We use these statistics only to understand and improve the game. We don't use them for advertising, we don't sell them, and we don't share them with anyone except PostHog, which processes them for us. We keep them only as long as they help us improve the game.

Because the statistics aren't connected to a person, we can't look up a particular player's records. Deleting the game resets the random ID, so new records can't be connected to old ones.

Purchases

Coin packs and the Starter Pack are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy. The game receives a receipt from Apple saying what was bought, so it can deliver it once, and keeps that receipt's ID with your progress on your iPhone. We never receive your name, your Apple Account or payment details.

Apple's parental controls work as usual: with Ask to Buy, a parent approves each purchase, and Screen Time can turn purchases off entirely.

Notifications

If you turn it on, the game sends one notification, "Your lives are full", when lives have refilled while the game is closed. It's scheduled on your iPhone itself; no server is involved, and we don't collect any notification token. Turn it off any time in the game's Settings (Lives reminder) or in the iPhone's Settings app.

Children

Dream Treehouse is rated 4+ and made for a general audience, including families. It has no ads, no chat, no accounts, no way to share content with other players, and no links out of the game except these support and privacy pages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous gameplay statistics described above contain no name, contact details or location and are used only to keep the game working well and improve it, never for advertising.

Parents: if you have questions about your child's use of the game, please contact us below.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn the lives reminder on or off in the game's Settings.
  • Turn off purchases with Screen Time, or require approval with Ask to Buy.
  • Delete the game to delete your progress from the iPhone and reset the random statistics ID.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. We hold no names or contact details, and the statistics can't be tied to a person, but you're welcome to contact us with any request or question. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it to process the anonymous statistics. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

These web pages

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no outside fonts or scripts. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep basic server logs for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Changes

If the game starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches players.
