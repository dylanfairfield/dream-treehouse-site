Help and Support

Answers to the questions we hear most. Settings are behind the gear on the home screen (and in the pause menu during a level).

How do I get my purchases back?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. It brings back the Starter Pack on a new iPhone or after reinstalling the game, using the same Apple Account it was bought with. Coin packs are used up when they're bought, so there's nothing to restore for them; the coins themselves travel with your progress (see the next question).

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Your treehouse is saved on your iPhone, not in an account. If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or move your data straight from your old iPhone, your progress comes along. Deleting the game deletes its progress, so please don't delete it before moving to a new iPhone.

How do lives work?

  • Levels 1 to 10 never cost a life.
  • After that, you have up to 5 lives. Losing a level, or leaving one before it's finished, uses one.
  • A life comes back every 30 minutes, or you can refill them all with coins.
  • Some treehouse chests give unlimited lives for a while.

What notifications does the game send?

Only one, and only if you turn it on: "Your lives are full", when your lives have refilled while the game is closed. Turn it on or off with Lives reminder in the game's Settings, or in the iPhone's Settings app under Notifications.

A purchase says "Waiting for approval"

That's Apple's Ask to Buy: a parent or guardian approves the purchase on their device, and the coins arrive in the game as soon as they do. If a purchase you made doesn't arrive, close the game and open it again with an internet connection.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Coins already delivered stay in the game.

I'm stuck on a level

Wait a few seconds and the game shows a possible move. Boosters (the Mallet, Leaf Blower, Garden Hose and Shake the Tree) help with tricky spots, and every one comes with free uses when it unlocks. Making two specials and swapping them together is the biggest help of all.

Sound, music and vibration

Music, sounds, haptics and reduce motion each have their own switch in Settings.
