Dream Treehouse

Build the treehouse you always wanted, one match at a time.

Dream Treehouse is a match 3 puzzle game for iPhone. Swap tiles, win levels and earn stars, then spend them to build a treehouse that climbs a giant tree, from a rope ladder at the roots to a garden in the sky.

150 levels, 12 treehouse areas, no ads and no account. Coming soon to the App Store.
